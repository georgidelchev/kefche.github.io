# word-chain: credits

Every asset and content source with its license (SPEC §19).

- **Rules:** the traditional word-chain game (also known as last-letter games), which nobody owns; the lives, the 80-word limit and the design are kefche's own.
- **Dictionary:** the shared word lists in `packages/lexicons` (ENABLE, public domain; BgOffice, MPL 1.1; the common-word lists the bots draw from), with their credits in `packages/lexicons/CREDITS.md`.
- **Art:** drawn in code for kefche (`assets/art.mjs`, with `scripts/game-art/kit.mjs`).
- **Sounds:** synthesized in code by the shared game SDK (`packages/game-sdk/src/browser/synth.ts`); no recordings or third-party audio.

No third-party assets beyond the word lists above.
