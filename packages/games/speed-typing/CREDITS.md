# speed-typing: credits

Every asset and content source with its license (SPEC §19).

- **Rules:** a typing test, which nobody owns; the scoring and design are kefche's own.
- **Classic passages** (`content/en.json`, `content/bg.json`, each with its author and work): from works in the public domain.
  - English: Jane Austen, Charles Dickens, Herman Melville, Lewis Carroll, Mary Shelley, Charlotte Brontë, Mark Twain, H. G. Wells, Arthur Conan Doyle, Leo Tolstoy (Constance Garnett's translation, 1901), Oscar Wilde, Kenneth Grahame and Louisa May Alcott, checked word for word against the Project Gutenberg texts.
  - Bulgarian: Hristo Botev, Ivan Vazov, Peyo Yavorov and Aleko Konstantinov, checked word for word against Bulgarian Wikisource (bg.wikisource.org), which marks them as public domain.
  - Typography is simplified so every character is on a keyboard: an ellipsis as three dots, dashes as hyphens, quotation marks as plain double quotes. Wording and spelling are the source's own.
- **Other passages:** written for kefche, in English and Bulgarian; released with the project.
- **Art:** drawn in code for kefche (`assets/art.mjs`, with `scripts/game-art/kit.mjs`).
- **Sounds:** synthesized in code by the shared game SDK (`packages/game-sdk/src/browser/synth.ts`); no recordings or third-party audio.

No third-party assets beyond the public-domain passages above.
